The strategic read

You do not have a national go-to-market. You have a Toronto go-to-market with a Vancouver follow-on. Toronto alone is $3.83M of SAM — 62× your year-one revenue. Spending sales hours outside the top two metros before saturating them is a rounding error dressed up as expansion. It also concentrates the SEO problem: win Persian search in the GTA and you have won 55% of the market.

Validation

Four ways this model could be wrong

A market size nobody stress-tests is a number, not an argument. Here is where this one holds and where it does not.

Holds up

Competitor listing counts bracket the TAM

gooyalisting indexes 7,471 listings, taablo 1,928, jabeh ~2,000, bazareyan ~1,000, iranianbusinesses 828, bazaarche 680. Deduplicated, the whole competitive set covers roughly 12,000–18,000 unique businesses. A 21,000 TAM sits just above that — which is correct, since no directory captures everyone.

Assumption, not data

The ownership rate is borrowed

11.9% is the all-immigrant average from StatCan's 2016 study. Nobody has published an Iranian-specific rate. StatCan does note that source-region variation is small except for Latin America and Sub-Saharan Africa, which supports using the average — but "supports" is not "measures."

The weak joint

Willingness to pay is untested

The SAM assumes 45% of businesses would pay $490–$2,700/year. Zero real businesses have been asked at those prices. Hamvatan failed at $290/yr — that is a value floor, not a price ceiling, but it is still the only local evidence you have, and it points the wrong way until you sell one.

Read this twice

$22M TAM is latent, not transacting

Total money currently changing hands in Persian-Canadian directory advertising is likely under $2M a year — most competitors are stalled, free, or dead. You are not capturing an existing budget; you are moving spend out of Google Ads, Instagram, and Persian print. That is a longer sale than a market-size chart suggests.

What to do with this

The market is not the bottleneck

Three conclusions follow directly from the arithmetic, and only one of them is about market size.

ConclusionWhy the numbers say so
Stop sizing, start pricingSOM is 0.9% of SAM. Even tripling the market changes nothing — you cannot sell the market you already have. The only lever that moves year-one revenue is ARPU, which is why $214 has to become ~$1,056.
Toronto first, alone55% of SAM is in one metro. A second city before Toronto is saturated splits scarce sales hours across two SEO fronts and two verification backlogs.
Sales capacity is the asset to buyAt $1,056 ARPU, one part-time commission seller who closes 60 accounts adds $63K of revenue against a fraction of that in commission. That is the highest-return spend available — higher than any product feature on the roadmap.
